Family Care

Family Care helps households build a preventive plan before a beloved stuffie reaches crisis. It is practical, gentle, and built around the real comfort role each stuffie plays.

New Companion Baseline

Document ideal loft, safe cleaning, and first signs of fatigue.

Shared Stuffie Protocol

Care plans for siblings, classrooms, waiting rooms, and therapy spaces.

Seasonal Review

Prepare for vacations, camp, holidays, and high-hug months.

Recommended Schedule

Most high-use stuffies benefit from a six-month preventive visit. Stuffies with travel, classroom, or bedtime duty may need quarterly review.
