Emergency Fluff

Emergency Fluff is for sudden changes that disrupt comfort right now: a torn seam before bedtime, a missing inner support, a collapsed ear, or acute stuffie worry.

Start urgent intake

When to Use Urgent Care

  • New opening with visible stuffing.
  • Stuffie cannot sit, snuggle, or be held in the usual way.
  • Important bedtime, travel, or recovery role is at risk.
  • Family has tried rest and the issue is worsening.
