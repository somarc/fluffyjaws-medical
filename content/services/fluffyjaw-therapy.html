FluffyJaw Therapy

FluffyJaw Therapy restores the comfort role of a stuffie when daily hugs, sleep routines, travel, or storage have changed the way it feels.

Request therapy consult

Who Benefits

This service is designed for stuffies with mild to moderate flatness, uneven cuddle response, early matted muzzle patterns, or a family report that the stuffie "just does not feel like itself."

Evaluation
Texture mapping, seam stress review, family comfort history, and FluffyJaw role scoring.
Clinic Treatment
Targeted brush-out, loft redistribution, inner support tuning, and guided handling practice.
Home Plan
Seven-day cuddle pacing, cleaning limits, and signs that a follow-up is needed.
