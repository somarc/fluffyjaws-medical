Button Eye Strain

Button Eye Strain is a visible loosening or tilt around a stuffie's eye area, often linked to travel, tugging, or repeated face-down sleep.

Early

Slight tilt, no loose thread, normal comfort role.

Watch

Thread movement, snag history, or family worry.

Urgent

Missing button, exposed attachment, or sharp edge.

What Not To Do

Do not pull, trim, glue, or hide the eye under heavy fabric. Save loose parts and request a care review.
