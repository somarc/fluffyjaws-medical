Matted Muzzle Syndrome

Matted Muzzle Syndrome appears when repeated comfort contact compresses surface fibers around the face, changing how a stuffie looks and feels.

Common Signs

  • Flattened muzzle fibers after brushing.
  • Patchy softness around the cheeks or chin.
  • Family reports that kisses feel scratchy or unfamiliar.
Mild
Use low-friction rest, gentle hand smoothing, and reassess in one week.
Moderate
Book FluffyJaw Therapy for texture mapping and controlled brush-out.
Severe
Avoid aggressive combing and request a clinic review to protect fabric integrity.
