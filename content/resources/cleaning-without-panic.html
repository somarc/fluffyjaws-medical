Cleaning Without Panic

Cleaning should preserve the comfort role, not reset the stuffie's whole personality. Start small, test gently, and avoid heat unless a care label clearly allows it.

Spot
Blot with a barely damp cloth and stop before the fabric feels saturated.
Rest
Let the stuffie air dry on a towel with its usual shape supported.
Review
If texture changes after cleaning, schedule a FluffyJaw Therapy review.
