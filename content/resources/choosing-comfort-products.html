Choosing Comfort Products

Comfort products work best when they support the care plan instead of replacing it. Use this guide to match fictional catalog items to the stuffie's current condition, handling tolerance, and bedtime role.

Start With The Care Signal

Pick the product family that matches the primary signal you are seeing at home.

  • Flatness after heavy use: start with recovery wraps or nap support.
  • Uncertain comfort changes: use a monitoring tool before changing routines.
  • Post-therapy rest: choose gentle support that reduces squeezing pressure.
