Care That Starts With the Stuffie

Every care plan begins with a story: where the stuffie sleeps, who carries it, how often it is hugged, and what kind of comfort it is expected to provide. From there, our clinicians match a FluffyJaw Fluffie to the right level of reinforcement, softness, and recovery support.

How Visits Work

Assess
We listen to the family, inspect texture, map high-stress cuddle zones, and identify the FluffyJaw role the stuffie needs most.
Treat
Care may include fluff balancing, seam support, gentle brushing, rest protocols, and replacement FluffyJaw matching.
Follow Up
Families leave with a care card, cleaning rhythm, hug-intensity guidance, and signs that the stuffie needs review.
